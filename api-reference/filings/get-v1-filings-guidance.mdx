Management guidance

<Info>
Audience: application and coding agent.
</Info>

## Coverage and source scope

Lists persisted management-guidance records extracted from covered earnings materials and transcripts, including metric, direction, target period, and disclosed ranges where available. These records are source-derived extractions, not normalized estimates, and do not amount to a full guidance history.

## Coverage and interpretation

Records are management-guidance extractions from covered earnings materials and transcripts, carrying metric, direction, target period, and disclosed ranges where stated. They are source-derived, not normalized estimates. Preserve citations and verify issuer definitions, units, and assumptions in the original disclosure.

## Canonical metadata

- `id`
- `ticker`
- `cik`
- `accessionNumber`
- `fiscalPeriod`
- `guidancePeriod`
- `metric`
- `direction`
- `valueLow`
- `valueHigh`
- `requestId`
- `traceparent`

## Example request

<RequestExample>
```bash
curl -X GET \
  -H "x-api-key: $SECAPI_API_KEY" \
  -H "secapi-version: 2026-03-19" \
  "https://api.secapi.ai/v1/filings/guidance"
```
</RequestExample>

## Failure posture

- a `404 filings_intelligence_not_available` on the guidance store means filing intelligence is turned off for the account, not that the query matched nothing; it does not describe the endpoint's current state
- an empty `data` array from the guidance store is a coverage gap for that issuer or window, not an outage
- preserve `requestId` and `traceparent` in logs and downstream reports
