SEC API is pay as you go. There is no monthly minimum and no setup fee. New checkout is Sandbox, Pay as you go, or Custom. The [public pricing page](https://secapi.ai/pricing) is the buyer-facing comparison; `GET /v1/billing` and `GET /v1/limits` describe the account that will run the work.

## Three columns

| | Sandbox | Pay as you go | Custom |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Price | Free | No monthly minimum | [Contact](https://secapi.ai/contact) |
| Users | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited, plus SSO + SCIM |
| Keys | Test keys, `livemode=false` | Production keys | Production keys, consolidated billing |
| Usage | 100 included calls each month. Test traffic is free. | Prepaid wallet at published list prices | Volume pricing beside published bundles |
| Rights | Evaluation | Internal use | Redistribution, embedding, SLA, dedicated TAM |

Seats are not a SKU. Keys are not a SKU. MCP, webhooks, streams, factors, macro, and intel use the same wallet.

## What you pay for

Published list, not meter-family jargon. After a prepaid bundle, the same list is discounted by the bundle's savings rate.

| Job | List |
| --- | --- |
| Resolve | `$0.01` |
| Filing / fact / statement / letter thesis / situation row | `$0.03` |
| Factor / macro | `$0.05` |
| Semantic search | `$0.08` |
| Extract / compare / letter document | `$0.10` |
| Intel pack (named job) | `$0.50` |
| Intel report (named job) | `$1.50` |
| Artifact | `$0.25` |
| Delivery event | `$0.001` |
| Notification email | `$0.005` |

Intel is a named job, not another store read. Packs are `deal_brief`, `letter_brief`, `filing_brief`, and `move_brief`. Reports are `country_report`, `portfolio_review`, `strategy`, and `underwriting_pack`. There is no open prompt. See [named intel jobs](/named-intel-jobs).

MCP is a channel. An intel pack through MCP is `$0.50`. An intel report through MCP is `$1.50`.

## Prepaid savings

Credits land at face value. The card charge is discounted. Copy is savings, not bonus lots.

| Credits in wallet | Save | You pay |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `$50` | 10% | `$45` |
| `$100` | 10% | `$90` |
| `$250` | 20% | `$200` |
| `$1000` | 30% | `$700` |
| Other (`$10`–`$5000`) | band below | live quote |

Checkout shows **Pay `$45` for `$50` in credits · Save 10%.** Other uses the same bands so a typed amount is not worse than the nearest preset.

| Credits requested | Save | You pay |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `$10`–`$49.99` | 0% | face (minimum top-up `$10`) |
| `$50`–`$249.99` | 10% | 90% of face |
| `$250`–`$999.99` | 20% | 80% of face |
| `$1000`–`$5000` | 30% | 70% of face |

Paid lots do not expire. Unspent credits can be refunded for 24 hours, in cash paid, fees non-refundable. After 24 hours they are non-refundable except for a billing error or as required by law.

Auto top-up uses the same table on the recharge face amount (monthly or balance threshold, not weekly). A `$100` auto top-up pays `$90` and credits `$100`.

There is no monthly per-meter volume curve. Rates below the `$1000` / 30% band are Custom.

## What is not billed

Health, quote, limits, catalog, test-key traffic (`livemode=false`), starter-grant resolve, MCP tool discovery, and `4xx` / `5xx` that fail before work starts. Failed, cancelled, or `4xx` intel jobs are free. Delivery receipts that are not a customer webhook are free. MCP discovery is free; the underlying job still bills.

## Sandbox

No card. Unlimited users. Test keys stay free. New organizations receive `100` included calls each month. The sandbox grant has 10 AI-query trial uses per UTC month. Production overage starts when you add PAYG credits or a card with auto top-up.

## Pay as you go

No monthly minimum. No setup fee. Prepaid wallet at the list above. Unlimited users. Unlimited keys within abuse limits. Auto top-up on balance threshold or monthly. Enable it in the signed-in [dashboard](https://secapi.ai/app). Until payment is confirmed, a billable request can return `402 billing_required`.

Self-serve PAYG is `internal_use`. A `200` from the API is not a redistribution license.

## Custom

Volume pricing beside or below published bundles, consolidated billing, dedicated technical account manager, SSO + SCIM, commercial redistribution / embedding, and SLA. [Contact](https://secapi.ai/contact).

## Grandfathered subscriptions

Pro (`$49` / month), Team (`$199` / month), and Commercial as a listed price floor are no longer sold. Existing subscriptions continue on their current terms and are not migrated mid-cycle.

## Rights

Sandbox and PAYG cover evaluation and internal use. Redistribution, embedding, white-labeling, resale, and Bulk Factor Export require Custom. Do not infer those rights from a successful request.

For a bounded workload, continue with [Pay as you go](/pay-as-you-go) or [billing-aware agent runs](/agent-billing).
